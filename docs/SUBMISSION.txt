=== 1. Two links, one paragraph (English) ===

Group 16's project is delivered as two live links, neither of which requires a login. The Git demo is the full web prototype at https://freefish0904.github.io/freefish-campus-guide/ , where you type a sentence such as "我在图书馆入口，怎么去 LG1 食堂？" and get a numbered indoor route drawn on a floor plan that switches floors as the walkthrough progresses. The Dify demo at https://udify.app/chat/ZAjpRIuJBp2fNQRk is the same application as a chat assistant: the identical routing engine is bundled into a Dify Code node, so every floor, lift number and walking time is still computed by our own JavaScript, and the language model is only invoked for sentences the rules genuinely cannot parse. Because both links run the same engine, they answer identically — ask the same question in both and compare.

=== 5. Three-sentence blurb ===

Freefish Campus Guide is a browser prototype that turns one sentence such as "我在图书馆正门，怎么去 LG1 食堂？" into a numbered indoor route: which lift to take, which floor to cross on, and which skybridge or covered walkway links the buildings. It understands English, Chinese and Cantonese (including typos) and plans on a real graph of corridors, stairs and six lift shafts, with a step-free mode that refuses stairs and compares its cost against the fastest route. Every answer is drawn on a floor plan that switches floors by itself during the walkthrough, and the whole interface can be re-read in the other two languages without losing the route.
